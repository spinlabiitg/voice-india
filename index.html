Lend Your Voice
by SPIN Lab, IIT Guwahati

Every district has a voice.
Light up yours.

We show you two everyday things. For each, say what you call it in the language you speak at home, then a sentence or two using that word. It takes a minute or two, and each voice adds a light to your district.

Or tap a district on the map.

Mock-up · demo counts · nothing leaves your device SPIN Lab, IIT Guwahati

About

Lend Your Voice is built by SPIN Lab (Sensing, Perception & INtelligence Lab), Mehta Family School of Data Science and AI, IIT Guwahati, as part of Bahubhashi India, a map of India's spoken languages.

Questions about the project? Write to neerajs@iitg.ac.in.
